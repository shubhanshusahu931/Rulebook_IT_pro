# Intentional Rulebook Contradictions

## Conflict 1 — Attendance
- §1.1 — 75% attendance required for examination eligibility.
- §2.3 — Approved medical exemption allows eligibility at 60%.

## Conflict 2 — Attendance Waiver
- §1.1 — Establishes the ordinary 75% attendance requirement.
- §3.1 — Academic Committee may waive the ordinary requirement.

## Conflict 3 — Tuition Deadline
- fee_deadlines.csv — Tuition deadlines include 15 August and 20 August.
- special_notices.pdf §11.2 — 2026-27 tuition deadline is 18 August.

These contradictions are intentional test cases.
The system should identify and display conflicting evidence instead
of silently choosing one rule.
